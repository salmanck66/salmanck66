### About me

I own and engineer **[JCClub](https://jcclub.in)**, a WooCommerce football-jersey store that ships orders across India every day. Most of the store runs on plugins I wrote myself: shipping automation, order tracking, GST invoicing, partial payments, OTP login, search and marketing. Before that I built Shopify apps at a product company, and I take on freelance builds for local businesses in Kerala.

What I enjoy most is the unglamorous, production side: payment flows, courier APIs, data migrations, caching and the bugs that only appear with real customers.

### Problems I've solved in production

- **Shipping automation.** A courier-API integration for AWB generation and rate comparison; tracking sync every 6 hours with multiple AWBs per order, exception/RTO detection and a stuck-pickup monitor.
- **Payments.** A 20% advance + COD partial-payment system. Found and patched security issues in a third-party payment integration, and fixed a compounding calculation bug in partial payments.
- **HPOS migration.** Resolved order-data divergence between WooCommerce HPOS tables and legacy post meta across a whole plugin stack.
- **GST compliance.** An invoice plugin with PIN-code-to-state resolution through the India Post API, with a circuit breaker to survive shared-hosting PHP timeouts.
- **Conversion and UX.** OTP login that auto-creates accounts for guest buyers and links their past orders; fuzzy search with typo correction; restock alerts; a size-level deals engine for clearing in-hand stock.
- **Performance.** A full-site audit covering plugin cleanup, cache-exclusion fixes, a CDN rollout and moving WP-Cron to a system cron.
